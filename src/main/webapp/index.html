How an exchange works

  1. List your books

    Add the title, edition, condition and what you would like in return.

  2. Find what you need

    Search by title or author and filter by subject or condition.

  3. Send a request

    Offer one of your own books and add a message to the owner.

  4. Meet and hand over

    The owner accepts, you meet on campus, and the exchange is marked completed.

Built for a campus

One request per book

Once an owner accepts a request, the book is reserved and competing requests are closed automatically.

Your history is kept

Every request, acceptance and completed exchange stays on record so nothing gets lost.

Only you edit your books

Listings can be changed or removed only by the student who posted them.
